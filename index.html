Questions

Good questions, short answers.

Who is KLIMB for?

Marketers at every stage: students and career switchers, juniors who want to move faster, and leads preparing for their next title. You tell us where you are and we start you at the right level.

How does the €1 offer work?

The first 1,000 subscribers get their first month for €1, with access to 2 kingdoms of their choice. After that it moves to Pro at €7.99 a month or €67.12 a year, unless you switch or cancel.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel in your app store account at any time, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

When does the app launch?

We're finishing the iPhone and Android apps now. You can play the full demo in your browser today. Write to us and we'll let you know on launch day.

Can you build a kingdom for my company?

Yes, that's Klimb Kingdoms. We design onboarding, training and academies around your content, your brand and your own mentor. Write to hello@klimb.academy.

Who made KLIMB?

KLIMB is made in Hamburg by marketers who have run creative teams, launches and budgets for over ten years, and who wished learning the job felt this good.
